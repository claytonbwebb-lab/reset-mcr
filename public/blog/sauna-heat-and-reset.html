Sauna, steam and heat: a simple guide to switching off properly

Heat is one of the simplest ways to slow down. Sauna and steam sessions create a clear pause in the week: phone away, breathing settled, muscles warming and the body moving out of work mode.

Why it matters

Regular heat sessions can support relaxation, mobility routines and post-training recovery habits. They are not a replacement for sleep, hydration or sensible training, but they can make those basics easier to stick to.

How RESET approaches it

RESET MCR is built around that idea of repeatable recovery: heat, cold, calm and routine, all in a space that feels premium without feeling complicated.
