Why a fresh cut belongs in your routine

A good barber appointment changes how you carry yourself. It sharpens your appearance, clears your head and sets a tone for the days ahead. Put it into a regular routine and the effect compounds — you look better, feel more dialled in, and people notice.

Why it matters

Most men underestimate what a proper haircut does. It's not just about looking tidy — it's about standards. A clean fade, a sharp line-up, a beard that's actually shaped rather than just trimmed — these things signal attention to detail. They affect how you walk into a room.

At Reset MCR, we treat every appointment as an opportunity to get you looking exactly right. Not rushed, not generic — exactly right.

How often should you get your hair cut?

It depends on your style and how fast your hair grows, but for most skin fades and short styles, every 2–3 weeks keeps things crisp. Longer styles can go 4–6 weeks. Talk to Jack or Jaden at your appointment — they'll give you honest guidance on what works for your hair and your look.

What to ask for

Bring a reference photo. Describe the kind of look you want, not just the length. The best appointments happen when you communicate what you're aiming for and trust your barber to execute it. If you don't know what you want, describe the lifestyle — busy, active, professional — and let them recommend.
