Why cold plunge recovery is becoming part of the modern training routine

Cold plunge recovery is not about trying to be extreme. For most people, the value is in creating a short, repeatable reset: a few minutes of cold exposure, controlled breathing and a clear break between training, work and the rest of the day.

Why it matters

Cold water can help people build a more deliberate recovery routine. It encourages slower breathing, a calmer nervous system response and a stronger sense of discipline. Used sensibly, it can sit alongside sauna, mobility, hydration and sleep as part of a wider wellbeing habit.

How RESET approaches it

At RESET MCR, cold plunge will be part of a broader recovery experience rather than a standalone gimmick. The aim is to make it approachable, structured and easy to build into a weekly routine.
