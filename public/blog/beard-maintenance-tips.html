5 beard maintenance tips every man should know

From daily washing to professional shaping — here's how to keep your beard looking its best.

A well-kept beard frames your face and adds character. But left untamed, it can quickly look messy and unkempt. Whether you're growing your first beard or you've had one for years, these five tips will keep it sharp.

1. Wash it properly

Regular shampoo strips your beard of its natural oils, leaving the skin underneath dry and itchy. Use a dedicated beard wash or a mild, sulphate-free cleanser 2–3 times a week. On off days, just rinse with warm water.

2. Condition and oil daily

Beard oil isn't a luxury — it's essential. Apply a few drops after washing to soften the hair and moisturise the skin beneath. For longer beards, a beard balm adds hold and keeps stray hairs in place. Look for natural ingredients like jojoba, argan, and vitamin E.

3. Comb and brush regularly

Combing trains your beard to grow in the direction you want and prevents tangles. Use a wooden comb (gentler on hair) in the morning and a boar bristle brush before bed to distribute oils and exfoliate the skin.

4. Trim every 2–4 weeks

Even if you're growing it out, regular trimming keeps the shape clean and removes split ends. Use sharp scissors for detail work and a quality trimmer with guards for the cheeks and neckline. The neckline is key — too high and it looks unnatural; too low and it looks unkempt. A good rule: follow the natural curve where your neck meets your jaw.

5. See a barber for the shape

Professional beard shaping makes a huge difference. A barber will create clean cheek lines, a sharp neckline, and a balanced silhouette that suits your face shape.
