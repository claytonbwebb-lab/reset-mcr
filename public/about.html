About us

About Reset MCR

Premium barbering under the railway arches in Stalybridge. Founded by Jack, built on precision.

Reset MCR started with a simple idea: Stalybridge deserved a barbershop that treated every cut like craftwork. Not a conveyor belt. Not a ten-minute rush. A place where you walk in looking however you look, and walk out sharp.

What we do

We specialise in skin fades, classic cuts, beard trims, cut-throat shaves and kids' cuts. Every appointment includes a complimentary drink and hot towels — because the experience matters as much as the cut.

Where we are

Unit 10, Waterloo Court, Waterloo Road, Stalybridge, SK15 2AU — right under the railway arches. Easy to find, hard to forget.

The team

Jack — Founder and lead barber. Years of experience in precision cutting, skin fades and modern styling. Jack sets the standard.

Jaden — Trainee barber. Learning the craft under Jack's guidance, specialising in fades and beard work.

Booking

Book online 24/7, choose your barber, pick your time, and get an instant confirmation. We also offer recurring bookings — weekly, fortnightly or monthly — so your slot is always locked in.
